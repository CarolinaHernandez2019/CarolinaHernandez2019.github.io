Information dashboards for the Youth Subdirectorate

I design information dashboards for the Youth Subdirectorate of the District Secretariat of Social Integration. Each one answers a different question: where to prioritize, how the media talks about youth, and how the youth public policy is progressing.

Prioritization dashboard for youth programs in Bogota

Developed to support social program allocation at the District Secretariat of Social Integration. Identifies localities with the highest concentration of vulnerable youth based on SISBEN 2024 data and compares needs across territories.
